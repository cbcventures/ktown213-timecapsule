Orchid Fridays

ATTENTION EVERYONE!! Friday nights at Orchid is the new K-Town hotspot for the foreseeable future. Just the big names alone, our first night had the Wayans brothers (Shawn and Keenen) and Snoop Dog, our second night had rapper Nate Dogg and Lisa Leslie. We are trying to bring that hip hop celeb/artist/ball player feel to the asian/mixed party scene (something that has never happened before!!) and hope that we can count on your continued support. The line up for the next three weeks is as follows:

July 16 - NFL Party hosted Simeon Rice (Tampa Bay All Pro) July 23 - Tracy Murray (NBA) Party (there is a possibility that Shaq may host this party for Tracy) July 30 - Method Man and Redman party for their new television show. We are also trying to line up John Cho (American Pie, etc.) for his Harold and Kumar movie release party
