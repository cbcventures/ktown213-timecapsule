350Z

Ride Spotlight

2003 Nissan 350Z OWNER: ILON TYAN

SHOUTOUT: My one and only love Amina, great friends Grace and Davis, Kevin ( www.aznkid.com ) for the amazing pics, and an extra special thanks to all the companies and shops that helped me out in the whole process: CustomEnterprise ( www.customenterprise.com ), Bully Clutch ( www.bullyclutch.com ), VerticalDoors ( www.VerticalDoors.com ), and Tuner Works ( www.TunerWorks.com ). KP foreva!

Part I: The Specs

Exterior - Veilside Type 3 Body Kit - VerticalDoors Inc. Vertical Door Conversion - Wheels: 19x8.5"F 19x9.5"R Axis Hiro wrapped BF Goodrich KDW 245/35/19F and 255/35/19R - Red "Sparkle" Paint Job - H.I.D. Headlamps - PIAA H.I.D. L.E.D. Fog Lights

Engine/Performance - INJEN Cold Air Intake - NISMO Headers - NISMO Rad / Oil Cap - NISMO LSD - NISMO / JDM Horn - Front tower strut brace - Bully Stage 3 Clutch - KVR Cross Drilled Rotors - KVR Stainless Steel Braided Brake Lines - KVR Carbon Fiber Brake Pads (Front) - ICE Ceramic Brake Pads (Rear) - Custom Enterprise CF. Engine Covers

Interior - Custom fiberglass interior, paint matched to the exterior with silver accents - Redesigned Dash converting a double din cd/deck openning into a single din - Custom fiberglass/Plexiglas trunk enclosure paint matched to the exterior with silver accents - Plexiglas amp-rack and capacitor amp mount for the floating effect - Eurolite neons and strobes

In Car Entertainment / Misc Dress Up - Clarion VRX935VD head unit w/ 7" "flip out" touch screen LCD - Additional 7" LCD for Navigation and Vehicle Statistics - DVD-ROM for onboard computer and navigation - Sony PlayStation 2 - JL Audio, 12" W6v2 - Zapco Reference 350 amp - Zapco SLB-U pre-amp - 1.0 Farad Digital Capacitor - Phoenix Gold Wiring / Fuses - Dynamat - OPTIMA yellow top battery - NISMO Floor Matts - NISMO Clear Markers - Veilside Pedals

In the Mail - Carbon-Creations CF OEM Fenders / Nismo-Style SideSkirts
