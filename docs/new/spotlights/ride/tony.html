Q & A with Tony Kim...

Q: Tell us about your car Tony. What car do you drive and what are the specs?

A: It's a 2001 Toyota MRS. What do you want to know particularly about the car? I'll let the photos do the talking.

Q: When did you get involved with fixing up cars and what not?

A: My interests stems from the age of 15, but I didnt get involved in the industry. If thats what you would call it, until 17 or 18 years old.

Q: I heard you worked with the producers of The Fast and the Furious as consultants. Tell us about that experience.

A: No, I didnt work with the movie studio. I aided in friends that were involved in the 2 nd movie.

Q: What do you like about your car the most?

A: My wheels and tires. They make or break the car.

Q: Where has you car been featured?

A: My car's been featured on Super Street, Max Car UK, Maxi Tuning, and Playboy.

Q: what's your next car?

A: No question, an Infiniti G35.

Q: What are you going to do to it?

A: It's a secret... Youll find out when you see a nice white one on the road in Ktown.

Q: Lastly, where do you like to hang out in K-Town?

A: I like to hang around my house, it's in K-Town. LOL
