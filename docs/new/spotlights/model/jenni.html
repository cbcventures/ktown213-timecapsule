Q & A with Jenni Choi

Ktown213: Jenni, not a lot of people know about you in Southern California, please give us a quick intro. Jenni Choi : Hey everyone! My name is Jenni Choi! I was born in Korea, and when I was about eight years old I moved to Maryland with my parents and my older brother. Currently I'm a college student majoring in pharmaceuticals but I'm thinking about going into nursing as well.

KT213: Jenni, tell us how you got your start in import modeling. JC: Well, I got started first when I was in the 10th grade. I did a little bit of modeling for clothes and ads before, but I started modeling for import cars not that long ago. Maybe about 2 years ago for a friend's car. After that I started modeling for teams around the east coast.

KT213: That's great, you must have a lot of fun doing that. What do you do on your spare time? JC: On my spare time i like to shop and hang out with my friends.

KT213: What are your turn-on's in a guy? JC: A man who is smart, and someone I can have a conversation with about anything. Also he has to have patience. A guy with a humor is a plus, because I need a guy that can cheer me up when I'm feeling down. A guy with motivation and goals.

KT213: Hear that guys? Please take notes! Now Jenni, where can we see more of you? JC: Well this year you won't be seeing me much, but next year I will be attending more shows so please check me out!

KT213: And this is a question I ask all our fine ladies Jenni, state your measurements! JC: Ha ha, I'm 34-26-36

KT213: Well obviously I can't ask you what your favorite things are about K-Town, so what do you like about our site? JC: Everything! I'm so glad there's a website like this, and I'm honored to be featured on such a great site!

KT213: Thanks Jenni, and we hope you have a wonderful time doing what you do! JC: Thank you! Mad love to K-Town! *muah*
