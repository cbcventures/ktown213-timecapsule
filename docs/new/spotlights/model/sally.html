Q & A with Sally Yi

Ktown213: Sally, give us a short intro about yourself. Sally Yi: My name is Sally Yi, born and raised in Hawaii, on the beautiful island of O'ahu. I am full Korean standing at the height of 5'1 or so. I'm currently a student at a community college hoping to get into the industry of becoming a flight attendant, but only temporarily until I know what I really want to do.

KT213: How did you get into modeling? SY: By another beautiful model name Kara Navarro. She gave me the opportunity to model since she was currently with a popular crowd called "808speed" along with "Threatoptions."

KT213: Who are some of your favorite models? SY: Shauna Ko and Natasha Yi are beautiful Korean ladies.

KT213: Where do you see yourself in 5 years? SY: Striving for success, being successful, contributing to the world, knowing more about myself, excepting, intaking, and learning life and so much more!

KT213: For the dying men in K-Town, what are your stats! SY: I'm 5'1", 103 lbs, 34B-25-35, and I'm size 1-3.

KT213: Are you single? What do you look for in a man? SY: Yes, I'm single. I have been single for a quite some time now, ha ha. I love a guy with humor, being able to make me laugh will mean the world especially when I am down. A man who has motivation and goals. Men who are not shorter than me when I wear my heels, ha ha. A man who is patient. This is the one of the most important factors in being my lover because with patience, understanding and excpetance all falls in place.

KT213: Have you been to K-Town in LA? SY: I'm not sure, ha ha. I have been to the mainland twice. I was in Washington State when I was still an infant due to having a father in the military, and subsequently being stationed there. I've also been in LA on my mother's business trip for three days but I'm not sure if I was in K-Town. But I will tell you this much, drivers there in LA are crazy! Ha ha..

KT213: Anything you want to say to viewers of ktown213? SY: Yes, first off I want to thank ktown213 for having me on this site. I loved answering these questions. You guys rock and this page is full of beautiful people. Hopefully when I visit LA maybe being on the job as a flight attendant, I will come party with you folks. songs. It is very tough but we can do it.

KT213: Thank you for your time Sally, we wish you the best of luck! SY: Thank you ktown213!
