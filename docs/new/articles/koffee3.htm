New Korean name for condom too close for comfort

Plans to cease using the English word condom in favor of a more Korean term have been set aside. The Korean Anti-AIDS Foundation held a contest to rename the rubber prophylactic and came up with ae-pil instead of the commonly used word condom. Complaints due to the similarity of ae-pil and some surnames in Korea have halted plans to implement the use of the new term.
