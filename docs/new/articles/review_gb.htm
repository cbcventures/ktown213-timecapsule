Impaired DVD REVIEW

Impaired is the second installment to be released by the ever controversial Green Bottle Productions. Impaired is filmed on DVD quality film to compliment the natural ambiance of the Hawaiian Islands. The material featured on this film include the wild lifestyle of the Hawaii Import Car scene, the breath taking beauty of the Prestigious Models, insane coverage of the high impact sport of drifting, not to mention some highly controversial "underground" footage. Impaired is only available on DVD and includes lifestyle related materials like unreleased pro surfing clips, various B-Boy competitions, nightlife in Hawaii, various scenic shots and timelapses and a crazy soundtrack provided by highly acclaimed artist like the Black Eyed Peas, Kottonmouth Kings, Zion 1, Nippondamic and more! Impaired is actively advertised in all lifestyle related magazines in Hawaii. Impaired is also featured on the Green Bottle website which generates over 20,000+ hits a month! Featured Models: Playboy Cybergirl and January 2004 Import Tuner Cover Model Karouna Kay Sivilay, Prestigious Models - Kim Loan, Brenda Do, Lan Le, and Alice Lee. A sneak peek with Christine Mendoza and Mia Guzman, then go behind the scenes with Xhift Magazine. Drift Showoff: Mainstream Productions, On the Scene Video Magazine, and Kaizo Speedgear present the Falken Tire Drift Showoff in Honolulu, HI featuring D1 Drivers Kumakubo and Kazama going head to head against Hawaii's best drifters. SEMA Show: Fast cars, hot women, and some crazy drifting with some of the best D1 Drivers. Drift Session Hawaii: See some of Hawaii's best drifters in action! Extra: Crazy action stunts that you've never seen before! Along with some streetbike action, sport truck fun, and pro surfing that'll get you "IMPAIRED!"
