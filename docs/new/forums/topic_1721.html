LALate Launches "LALate New 2 Watch Chart"


Today LALate.com, the most successful company on nightlifestyle in LA, announced the launch of a new chart to track up-and-coming new VIP parties in LA that LALate predicts might be very hot.

LALate - with spinoff sites NYLate.com and TeenLate.com - is the preeminent authority on nightlifestyle with one of the fastest growing followings of clubgoers in the United States. In a word, as Zagat's is to restaurants and Frodor's is to travelling, LALate is to nightlifestyle.

But with so many events, nearly four dozen events on its calendar per week including A+ LA venues White Lotus, Shelter, and 9000 Lounge, LALate reduces those events to two of the most talked about charts in LA nightlifestyle: the "LALate 7-Day Planner Chart" and "LALate Hot @ 9 Chart". Today, LALate adds the brand new "LALate New 2 Watch Chart".

"We are getting so many new events weekly. We found ourselves writing a good amount almost daily to our clubgoers about these events that are up and coming, that may be extremely hot, but as of now, are still new events that we think people need to watch and try out. Hence, we decided to organize all that information into what we call the LALate New 2 Watch Chart, new events for LALate that people need to watch and try out."

The "LALate New 2 Watch Chart" joins the already online nationally syndicated and branded "LALate 7-Day Planner Chart" and "LALate Hot @ 9 Chart" that give up to the minute information about what's hot and what's not, along with several "LALate Best Of" award nominations announced by LALate throughout year for holidays (NYE, Halloween) and seasons (Summer, Fall).

The LALate 7-Day Planner, the most talked about event planner in LA, takes the best of all those parties and chooses which one venue you should party at each night. If you were to party at only one place, this is where you should go that night. Similarly, if you wanted to be at a special party at 9pm each night of the week, the LALate Hot @ 9 Chart lists those parties, special venues, or special one-time or generally nonconsecutive parties.

With a market saturation of readers estimated at over 85% of VIP clubgoers in Los Angeles, LALate's newsletter will celebrate its 100 edition next week.
