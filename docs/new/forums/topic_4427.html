Went there for new years, I thought it was pretty good. Upscale cafe with a club like aptmosphere. No dance floor, but everything is so spacious you could make one right next to your table if you were drunk enough. Food is good, and the alcohol isn't limited to beer and soju as all the other cafe's in the ktown collective are generally limited too. Orchid is similar to palm tree, but a bit darker, and much more appealing. They serve bottles of hard liquor in flights of shotglasses which I thought was pretty interesting--less stress in the wrists from the lack of the pouring action. Overall I give it a 7 out of 10. They also gave ddukgook on New Years, that was very good times.
