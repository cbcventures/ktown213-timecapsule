LALate Announces Two New LA Hot Spot Openings

LALateWed Nov 26, 2003 10:11 am
LALate.com, the most successful company on nightlifestyle in LA, this week will get introduce LA residents to, not one, but two new venue openings in Los Angeles. This follows six other venue openings LALate has carried in 2003, alone.

LALate - with spinoff sites NY Late and Teen Late - is the preeminent authority on nightlifestyle with one of the fastest growing followings of clubgoers in the United States. LALate is the first ever one-stop websites for residents to get into the most exclusive VIP-only parties listing several dozen events sometimes per night featuring top A+ celebrities, from mansion parties to top LA venues, to secret thousand person warehouse parties with acts that rival major stadium performances.

Tuesdays @
Pearl
Restaurant & Nightclub
665 North Robertson Boulevard
West Hollywood, CA

On Tuesday, LALate will introduce LA residents what it sees as the top contender for the #1 venue title in Los Angeles: the brand new ultra hot Pearl Restaurant and Nightclub. Earlier this month, Pearl management gave LALate a private tour of the new venue that had just hosted pop sensation Usher's birthday party.

Pearl is an exquisite, expansive venue that fills the void currently in LA for a completely upscale large capacity venue on the westside. At a time when #1 venue, White Lotus, has fallen off dramatically along with other previous hotspots 9000 Lounge and Shelter, Pearl is opening its doors with an unbelievable offering.

The 7,500 sq. foot venue located at 665 North Robertson Boulevard just south of Santa Monica Boulevard took recently housed popular restaurant nightclub Moomba and brought in upgrades that make Pearl a delightful experience. As a result, to no surprise, it has already hosted some of the hottest private parties in LA like ABC's Season Premiere Party for "The Bachelor'", the Wrap Party for "Scary Movie 3" , Usher's 25th Birthday Party, the "Gothika" Movie Premiere Party, and R. Kelly's Platinum Party. This Tuesday, Pearl is unveiled to LA clubgoers.

The nightclub section of Pearl offers a $250,000 system replete with six plasma screens, 100 speakers and two DJ stations, a 24-track recording board, a $45,000 Sony digital projector, and a 250 inch big screen that descends from the ceiling on the main stage.

In the main room, Pearl took out the booths to expand the dancefloor while adding intimacy through the addition of a pocket area off the stage. Shadow boxes offers an added treat as does a half-dozen screens that cross-pollinate the action in Pearl around the venue

In the VIP area downstairs, gone are the low back risers in a myriad of confusing colors from the Moomba era, and in are a sexy striptease poll surrounded with stunning high back suede sofas in a soothing palliate.

With nearly 7,500 square feet to operate, Pearl still maintains intimacy for dining and nightlife with an exceptional floorplan. Pearl chefs offer a modern American cuisine with an Asian flair, specializing in seafood with Asian twists.

Gloew Nightclub
Beverly Hills Loews Hotel
1224 S. Beverwil Drive
Beverly Hills, CA

Meantime, across town on Friday, LALate will ride the elevator to the brand new Club Gloew atop the chic Loews Beverly Hills Hotel.

Gloew is the first in a series of new hotel nightclubs that will appear on the scene in '03 and '04. Hotel venue always do well, including the Meridian's parties early this year.

With an expansive view of the city, Gloews will offer Hollywoods top funky house DJ, DJ Lee Dyson, who will spin todays hottest Hip-Hop, R&B, Funky House, and other rare grooves.

Both events top this week's nationally syndicated and branded "LALate 7-Day Planner Chart" and "LALate Hot @ 9 Chart" which give up to the minute information about what's hot and what's not in LA.
