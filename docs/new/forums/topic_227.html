Forum Rules & Guidelines

ktown213Fri Aug 22, 2003 9:18 pm
1. Please refrain from flaming, and blatant verbal attacks are not allowed. Be civil, and respect the feeling of others.

2. Excessive use of profanity is discouraged. That doesn't mean it can not be used, just use your own discretion.

3. No flooding the forums. Includes double-posting.

4. DO try to stay on topic from within a particular thread. And we realize that threads will naturally stray off-topic sometimes, so this is not a totally strict rule... just don't get carried away.

5. DO try to stay within the theme of a particular forum.

6. No SPAMMING. Violators will be banned.

7. No advertising. (Event/Party promoters may post information about events and parties in the Ktown Los Angeles Forum.)

8. No porn. Violators will be banned.

9. Have fun!

. . . .

The administrators and moderators reserve the right to take appropriate action in situations where a member does not comply with the above rules & guidelines.
