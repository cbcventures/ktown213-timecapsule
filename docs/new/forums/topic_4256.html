basically, it's a dutch auction. google will release 150 million shares in the auction. people bid...individuals bid, etc.

the 150 million shares for the IPO will go at the IPO price that will be determined by this auction.

say there are bids for 1 billion shares...the highest guy is willing to pay say $100,000 for 100 shares...the cheapest guy is willing to bid $5 for 10000 shares, etc.

well, they will start from the the top...and go down until 150 million shares are taken up. the ACTUAL price of the IPO will be the cost of the 150 million-th share. so if the guy that is 150 million down the line is actually $35.98, then the guy who bid $100,000 per share will get his 100 shares...at $35.98 each and not his $100,000 that he bid.
