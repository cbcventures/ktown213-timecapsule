7/22 TORO w/Michael Anthony & John Wander

Nightshade Music and Cyclotron Records bring progressive house to the Conga Room with top local jocks every Thursday.

This week, ROAM presents:
MICHAEL ANTHONY (Late Night Sneaky, Barouque)
John Wander (Roam, Electrophonic)

At 25, Michael Anthony, is actively pioneering a new chapter in US Electronica. His unconventional fusion of house, tech, polyrhythmic electro funk & Nu Skool Breaks, are coupled with undertones of classic rock & tribal percussion. With residencies at 1015 Folsom in San Francisco, Key Club in LA, and as a signed artist with Bedrock Records, Michael is one of the busiest and most eclectic DJ/ producers on the West Coast.



9PM - 2AM
