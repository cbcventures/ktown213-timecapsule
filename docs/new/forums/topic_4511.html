7-1-04 - Toro @ the Conga Room w/John Kelley, Treavor, FUz, Lord Runningclam, & Alex Spurkel

John Kelley & Treavor (Moontribe)
Ball of Waxx Record Release party with FUz, Lord Runningclam, and live drumming by Alex Spurkel

FUz - Resonation Restoration - Hypnotic chugging house, earthy psychadelic funk and ethereal moodiness; FUz never seizes to astonish. Hints of flamenco counterpoint infectious grooves. Goose-bumping synths spill into the air while tribal beats pump the bloodstream. Resonation Restoration peels back reality and opens up the imagination.

Brought to you by Cyclotron Records and Nightshade Music. Free w/RSVP $5 without. Special events will vary. Send an e-mail to guestlist@toroclubla.com to sign up.

Thanks to everyone who came out last week for Jason Blum of Deepsky and Brett King.
