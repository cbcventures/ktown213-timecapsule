LALate Launches Spring-LALate

As highschool and college students head on spring break this week, one company is set to put Los Angeles back on the mark as a top destination for spring break clubgoers.

LALate, the company that has the largest following of clubgoers in Los Angeles, this week launched an expansive campaign called Spring-LALate to carry never before seen parties in Los Angeles to show tourists how Los Angeles has grown to become one of the clubgoing capitals in the nation.

LALate carries nearly four dozen top events per week trhough LALate from #1 venues to secret warehouse parties. This Spring, LALate is carrying with its Spring-LALate campaign special one night parties perfect for Angelenos and travelers to experience a glimpse of nightlife unique to LA.

On March 20, LALate wil offer the mega Asian Models Bikini Party featuring nearly 100 girls and more in Wet-Shirt contests and more that rivels any fest in Cancun or Florida.

On March 27, LALate will offer a huge mulit-milliondollar Hollywood Hills Mansion open bar party with stunning views of the city.
