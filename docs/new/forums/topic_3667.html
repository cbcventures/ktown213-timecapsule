**TOKYO NIGHT 2004** on Saturday, March 27, 2004


Message: The 19th Annual **TOKYO NIGHT 2004** on Saturday,
March 27, 2004...

The Japanese Student Networks (JSN) presents The
19th Annual **TOKYO NIGHT 2004** on Saturday,
March 27, 2004 @ The Park Plaza Hotel from 9:00pm
to 4:00am!

This is the *BIGGEST* Japanese party sponsored by
JSN of USC, UCLA, UCSB, CSULB, and CSUSB. There
will be OVER 2000 people. Maximum capacity is
expected.

We will have *TOP* DJs from *Japan* spinning Hip-
Hop, House and Techno.
There are two *BIG* dance floors, a lounge area,
and Japanese food (yakitori, okonomiyaki, bento
boxes, etc.) provided by JSN and Gyukaku
(Japanese BBQ).

18and Over Party (Photo IDs are required)
Pre-Sale Tickets are $22.00
Pre-Sales VIP Tickets are $30.00

Address:
The Park Plaza Hotel
607 South Park View St.
Los Angeles, CA 90057

To purchase pre-sale tickets, please contact
Kaori at: kaori@dmkworld.com
Please visit: www.TKN2004.com for more
information.

Thank you and see you all there!
