LALate 7-Day Planner Chart - Top Parties this Week

LALateTue Mar 16, 2004 5:30 pm


LALate publishes three online nationally syndicated and branded charts that help rank parties in LA:

a) the "LALate 7-Day Planner Chart", which charts the single best party for each day of the week;
b) the "LALate Hot @ 9 Chart", which ranks additional hot parties each night; and
c) the new "LALate New2Watch Chart", which charts parties from less talked about venues and nights that may be the next great hot spot and need to be watched.



Every week we have nearly four dozen events on our calendar on lalate.com for you to attend. Our 7-Day Planner, the most talked about event planner in LA, takes the best of those parties and chooses which one venue you should party at each night. If you were to party at only one place, this is where you should go that night. And in a new feature, we name the best night that week to party.
