Weird guy at Bohemian

RVDMon Nov 10, 2003 12:47 pm
I was at Bohemian on Friday night. I haven't been there in a long time but a couple of girls I know started working there so I decided to go in, drink some scotch at the bar, etc.

Then a weird guy walks in. He's old...looks like he's around 50 years old, he's Korean and he's wearing a cheap suit. He walks in by himself, sits at the bar and orders a Hite beer. He doesn't say much but sits and slowly drinks his beer (pace was like 1 beer every 1.5 hours) and eats popcorn. After about 30 minutes he made some conversation with the Mexican busboy and they shared a drink.

I asked my bartender friend who this dude is and she said that he comes in "all the time" and only drinks like 1-3 beers. He nurses those drinks over about 3-4 hours. He always sits at that same seat at the bar because he can get a good view of the waitresses as they walk into the kitchen...he stares at their short skirts.

He also rarely tips. He usually tips about $1-2 but many times just walks out without leaving a tip. Neither her nor any of the other waitresses talked to the guy all night....and yeah, he was there until like midnight.

Anyone else see this weird guy? btw, the place was pretty dead.

I stopped by Bliss before going to Bohemian and my friend and I were sitting at the bar. But this waitress kept coming by and sticking her arm next to my friend's face so he got pissed and we left.

RVD.
