DDALA, the Designated Drivers Association, has joined the LALate listed Mansion Party tomorrow 3/27!

In December, 2003, DDALA and LALate made national news as Los Angeles' biggest nightlife company, LALate, joined forces with charity DDALA to prevent holiday drinking and driving. Together, they launched the largest campaigns ever in Los Angeles to prevent Holiday drinking and driving among Los Angeles youth. To date, the two companies are still aligned to prevent alcohol-related drunk driving accidents.

The DDALA Service

DDALAA offers the only service of its kind in LA to prevent alcohol-related accidents. On any Friday and Saturday night, DDALA will drive a person who has been drinking home in their own car free of charge. Supported by California's top elected officials and organizations including the DMV and Senator Barbara Boxer, DDALA year round dispatches through its toll free number fully insured drivers who will get Hollywood clubgoers home safely in their own car after a night of partying.
