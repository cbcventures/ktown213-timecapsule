Police sweep of K-town

dneroThu Mar 18, 2004 5:45 pm
Los Angeles police are planning a "task force" sweep of K-town Friday, March 19, through 4 a.m. Sat.

Although it's not a DUI-related sweep (see below), there will be tons of cops on the streets (usually more than 100 officers are assigned to such sweeps), so my suggestion is to keep your head up.

The LAPD started these neighborhood sweeps in the valley around the time of the proposed valley succession. In my view it was the department's way of hushing pro-succession critics who said the valley wasn't getting its fair share of city services. When the new chief (Bill Bratton) came in, he liked the sweeps and has begun doing them citywide (I think this is the first one outside the valley).

More info: http://www.lapdonline.org/press_releases/2004/03/pr04173.htm
