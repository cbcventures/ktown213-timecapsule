JT Donaldson has been producing since the birth of Dallas' Fairpark Records in 1997. He has produced deep, soulful house music for labels such as Cajual, Cyclo, Distance, Seasons, Push & Pull, and Viva to name a few. He has worked with artists like Lance DeSardi, CPEN, Tim Shumaker of Home and Garden, Sandy Rivera of K.O.T., Chris Nazuka, Lo Soul, The Freaks, and house legend Chez Damier.

As a co-founder and in house producer for Fairpark, JT moved to Chicago from Dallas after the label took a hiatus. After recording an album for Seasons in Los Angeles, he moved again, this time to the left coast where he was working with Lance DeSardi and Chris Penny aka CPENN. Currently he resides in Chicago where he has started his new record label with co-owner Tim Shumaker called, Gallery Music Group.

He has been able to share the music through DJing, producing, and now with his new label featuring remixers and artists like 2nd Shift, Home & Garden, Derrick Carter, Lance DeSardi, PTy LTd, Slater Hogan & John Larner, and Joshua.

JT’ s music has been in demand world wide and has taken him to places like South Africa, Australia, New Zealand, Malaysia, South Korea, Germany, Norway, France, Belgium, Turkey, The U.K. and many others.






SEASONS
Beautiful for the Ugly

Seasons, not just a company name, but a lifestyle, an illustration of how beautiful life really can be. Jamie Thinnes founded the multi-faceted company in 1996, his love affair with electronic music was the motivation behind the commencement of Seasons. His fascination of how a DJ could manipulate the moods of their listeners, loaned him the curiosity to dig deeper into the world of sound. He enrolled and graduated in a recording engineering program, refining his knack for sound design and structure. A short time after, he founded his now thriving Seasons Recordings imprint(formerly Earthtones).

Fast-forward ten years and you will find the name Jamie Thinnes, as a DJ that is aggressively sought after all over the world. He has toured throughout Northern America, Europe, South Africa, and most recently Australia. Jamie also is a resident DJ in Los Angeles, Orange County, Puerto Vallarta, Vancouver, and San Diego.

Seasons Recordings, record label, has become one of the most respected and desired labels in the house music industry. Jamie’s passion for blending organic style with an industrial undertone has been his baseline for not only his manner as a DJ, but as well as the benchmark sound stamped on each mindfully picked record. Season’s albums and artists include: Undercover Agency, 2nd Shift, Sumo, JT Donaldson, Derrick Carter, Lance DeSardi, DJ Heather, Joshua (a.k.a.: IZ), SWAG, Freaks, and other great works and musicians.

Jamie’s Success in the recording business aspired him to branch the label out in a new direction. He started Seasons clothing in the beginning of 2000. The clothing line follows the same characteristics of the natural flow of the Seasons label. With timeless pieces, clean streamlines and classic graphics. The Seasons theme, “Beautiful for the Ugly”, organic life fighting to survive and keep the manmade jungle alive and harmonious, is carried throughout the Seasons empire. With the pleased audience of Seasons ventures, the natural progression into newest element , Seasons Culinary, was formed. These are organized events held at first rate restaurants and lounges, for a night filled with beautiful music, delectable food, and cultivated patrons, to create an ambiance of overall pure enjoyment. Currently being held monthly at the Onyx Room in San Diego, and the original night of Chimayo’s in Huntington Beach, a weekly occasion, every Thursday.

Seasons believes in the evolution of change, and will continue to evolve and grow in all aspects that could use a touch of the company’s natural beauty.
